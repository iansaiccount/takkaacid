# Your first sound

Start it as [`02-install`](02-install.md) says. The window opens on a line that already plays: the **Slow Acid** demo, loaded under the
name a new song starts with, `SONG New song`.

Nothing about that line is on disk yet. The strip says so in one word, **`UNSAVED`**, sitting just right of the wordmark
`takka-acid`, with the gear and the `?` beside it. That word is the only thing on the face the machine uses to tell you
whether your work is safe, so learn to glance at it:

- `UNSAVED` — you have changed something since the last save. The session is written when you close the app, so a lost
  session means it was lost there — by a crash, or by a kill — not by leaving the window open.
- `SAVED` — what is on screen is what is on disk.

## Make it play

Press **`RUN`**, in the bottom row, or press the space bar — `SPACE` is the same key as `RUN` / `STOP`.

The line starts. `RUN` stays lit while it plays and `STOP` lights instead the moment you stop it. Press `SPACE` again to
stop: the line holds where it is, and the next start begins from the top of the bar.

That is the entire first lesson. The demo is a real pattern: you can pull it apart step by step, change the sound under
it, and keep it under your own name when you like it.

## Nothing here bites on the first click

The machine is sixty-four patterns and a handful of buttons, and it is easy to press the wrong one. So the destructive
things ask twice:

- **`CLEAR`** empties the pattern. The first click arms it — the key reads `CLEAR?` for a few seconds — and a second
  click inside that window empties it. Editing a step drops the arm: a confirmation is about the pattern as it was.
- **`NEW`**, in the songs card, throws away the session and starts from the starter phrase. While your session is
  unsaved it asks `SURE?` first.
- **Loading** a song over an unsaved session, and **deleting** a song, each take a second click as well.

Everything else — the knobs, the step keys, the transport, the pattern selector — is safe to touch. Turn a knob and see
what happens; the line is still playing.
